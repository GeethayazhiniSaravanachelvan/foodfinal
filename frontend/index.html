How It Works

Connecting Food Surplus with Community Need

Every day, good edible food goes unused while community members face hunger. Foodshare bridges this gap in three simple steps.

🏪

1. Donors Post Surplus

Restaurants, bakeries, markets, and caterers list surplus meals or groceries in seconds with portions, expiry deadlines, and pickup instructions.

🤝

2. Recipients Request Food

Local shelters, food pantries, and individuals discover active food donations nearby and request portions directly with transparent status tracking.

♻️

3. Zero Waste Handshake

Food is collected safely during the designated pickup window. Nutritious food feeds people instead of decomposing in landfills.
